# Sleeve: TCG Companion — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 24, 2026
**App:** Sleeve: TCG Companion (Android, package `com.stevie.tcgcompanion`)
**Developer:** Stevie D M Roberts III ("we", "us")
**Privacy contact:** Stevie.sleevetcg@gmail.com
**Website:** https://steviesleevetcg-rgb.github.io/Sleeve-legal/

## The short version

- Sleeve has **no accounts, no sign-in, no cloud sync and no server of ours**. We don't receive, see or store your card lists, decks, prices or life-counter history.
- Everything you enter stays **on your phone**, unless you choose to export, back it up or share it.
- To show cards, the app connects directly to public card-data and image services. Like any internet request, those services receive your device's **IP address** and basic technical details. For Magic, they also receive the **search text** you type.
- The QR scanner uses Google's **ML Kit** on your phone. Google says ML Kit doesn't send the camera images to Google, but it does send Google some **diagnostic and device information**.
- No ads are shown or requested in this version, and we use no analytics or crash-reporting service.

## 1. Information we collect

**We (the developer) collect no personal information.** Sleeve has no account system and sends nothing to us. We don't sell or share your personal information, and we don't use it for advertising.

## 2. Information stored on your device

Sleeve saves the following **only on your device**, in the app's private storage:

- **Your lists.** Your collection, trade binder, chase list, decks and folders. That covers card IDs, quantities, variants, sideboard flags, deck format and hero, the prices you typed in, and copies of card names and similar details, plus a link to each card's picture. The picture files themselves aren't saved.
- **Life counter history.** Up to 500 entries per game, plus any player names you type.
- **Settings.** For example spoilers, the games you added, backup reminders, the dates of your last backup and first use, and whether the camera explanation has been shown.
- **Recent searches.** Your last 3 Lookup searches for each game, so you can run them again. They stay on your phone and aren't sent to us.
- **A small image-link file.** For Lorcana, a small file of image links, kept for up to 24 hours. Card pictures and card data are loaded each time you need them and are held only in the phone's memory while the app is open. They aren't saved on your phone.

We can't see this information.

**Android device backup.** If you have Android's backup turned on, Android may include Sleeve's app data in your Google account backup. Google's documentation says this backup is stored in a private folder in your Google Drive and, on Android 9 and newer, is end-to-end encrypted with your screen lock. That backup is between you and Google; we can't access it.

## 3. Things you choose to share

These only happen when you tap them:

- **Back up and export.** "Back up now" creates a file such as `Sleeve backup 2026-11-18.json`, containing your lists and settings. "Export lists" creates CSV, JSON, PDF or spreadsheet files. You decide where the file goes, using Android's share sheet, file picker or print service. Whoever receives it can read it.
- **QR share codes.** A QR code holds the list's name, card numbers, counts and any prices you typed. It's shown on your screen for another phone to scan. Nothing is uploaded.
- **Restore and import.** When you pick a file, Sleeve reads only that file.
- **TCGplayer links.** Tapping 'View on TCGplayer' opens TCGplayer's website in your browser and searches for that card's name, so TCGplayer receives the card name. TCGplayer's own privacy policy applies there.

## 4. Third-party services the app connects to

Looking up cards and viewing My Cards need an internet connection. The life counter works offline. To load card data and images, Sleeve connects directly from your phone to the services below. Each one receives what any website receives from a visitor: your **IP address**, the request (which card, image or search), and technical details such as the app's user-agent Sleeve/ followed by the app version number, plus "(unofficial TCG companion app for Android)". Their own privacy policies apply. We don't control these services or receive anything from them about you.

|---|---|---|
| GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com) | Flesh and Blood card data each session; a connection check | — |
| Google (connectivitycheck.gstatic.com) | Checking you're online | — |
| Legend Story Studios' image host (legendstory-production-s3-public.s3.amazonaws.com, on Amazon Web Services) | Flesh and Blood card images | — |
| Scryfall (api.scryfall.com and Scryfall's image servers) | Magic card data and images | The **search text you type**; card names; the card IDs in a Magic list when you create or scan a QR share code for it |

Sleeve has **no ads** and no advertising code. If we ever add ads, we'll update this policy and ask for any consent the law requires first.

## 5. Permissions and why

- **Internet and network state.** Used to load card data and images, and to check whether you're online.
- **Camera.** Only for scanning QR share codes. Android asks for it the first time you use Scan. Frames are processed on the device and not saved. You can refuse or revoke it in Android settings; everything except scanning still works.
- Sleeve asks for no other permissions.

## 6. Children

Sleeve is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. It doesn't ask for anyone's age, has no accounts and sends us no personal information. If you think a child has given us personal information, contact us at Stevie.sleevetcg@gmail.com and we'll delete anything we hold. Normally we hold nothing.

## 7. How long data is kept and how to delete it

- **On your device.** Your data stays until you delete it. You can delete lists and entries in the app. To remove everything, uninstall Sleeve or go to Android Settings > Apps > Sleeve > Storage > Clear storage. **Deletion can't be undone**, so back up first if you want a copy.
- **Files you exported or shared.** Delete them wherever you saved or sent them.
- **Android backup.** Manage or delete it in your Google account or device backup settings.
- **Third-party services.** They keep their own server logs (for example IP addresses) under their own policies. We can't delete those for you.
- **With us.** We hold no copy of your data, so there's nothing for us to delete or export.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU or UK GDPR, or California law), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or move your personal information, to object to or restrict its use, and to complain to your data protection authority. Because your Sleeve data stays on your device, you can view, change, export ("Export lists" or "Back up now") and delete it yourself at any time. For anything else, contact Stevie.sleevetcg@gmail.com.

- **Selling or sharing.** We don't "sell" or "share" personal information as California law defines those terms.
- **"Do Not Track".** Sleeve doesn't track you across other apps or websites, so these signals don't change anything.
- **Third-party tracking.** Apart from the services in section 4 receiving their normal technical data, no other parties collect information about your activity across apps or websites through Sleeve.

## 9. Security

Card data and image requests use HTTPS. Your data is kept in Sleeve's private app storage, which Android protects from other apps. Exported and backup files aren't encrypted, so keep them somewhere safe.
