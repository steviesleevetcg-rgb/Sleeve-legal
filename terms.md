## 1. What Sleeve is

Sleeve is a free, unofficial, fan-made companion app for trading card games. It lets you look up cards, keep your own card lists (collection, trade binder, chase list and decks), type in your own trade prices, share lists by QR code, and use a life counter. It currently supports Flesh and Blood and Magic: The Gathering, and Disney Lorcana TCG.
